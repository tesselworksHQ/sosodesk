How to read this page
  1. The call (top left) is today's stance; paper position is what the desk holds after its risk limits, which can be smaller than what the signal wanted.
  2. Why shows the five inputs: each scores from −1 (risk-off) to +1 (risk-on), and the weighted mean is the composite.
  3. Evidence lists every number behind those scores with the SoSoValue request it came from, so you can check any of them.
  4. Execution shows what the same trade would cost on SoDEX's live order book right now.
  5. Track record keeps every call, the misses too. Each has a receipt (a hash of the call and its evidence); “on-chain” links show the transaction that timestamped it.

Rules and limits

  • Weights: US spot ETF flows 0.35, Price trend 0.25, SoSoValue index breadth 0.15, News tone (24 h) 0.15, Fear & Greed (contrarian) 0.10
  • Long-only spot BTC, never above 50% of equity; at most 20% of equity traded per day; 10 bp fee per trade.
  • Kill-switch: 10% below peak equity → flat for 3 days.
  • ETF flows and price trend disagree strongly → half size. High-impact macro event (CPI, FOMC, payrolls, PCE, GDP) today or tomorrow → half size. Missing ETF or price data → hold, no new risk.
  • No look-ahead: a call uses ETF days and closes strictly before its date and fills at that date's close. Replayed days (first run) use the same rules without news or index breadth, which the API does not serve historically.
  • Paper trading only. Not investment advice.
